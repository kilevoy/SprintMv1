# Cold-enclosure openings evidence

`STATUS = UNKNOWN for the general product domain`

`окно`, `дверь`, `ворота`, `проем`, `оконный ригель`, `стойка окна`, or strip
glazing framing. It therefore does not provide a legacy window-framing rule
and does not prove whether ordinary wall girts continue, split, or deduct at
openings in another project workbook.

not be generalized.
